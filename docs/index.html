DocAgent

Translate, question and summarize climate and energy reports (IPCC, IEA, French High Council on Climate), with sources checked before an answer is shown.

DocAgent translates a PDF without breaking its layout, answers questions on the reports with the page and the sentence that prove the answer, and hands longer requests to an agent that plans the steps and has every summary and comparison reviewed before it is shown. The code, the evaluations and the architecture are on GitHub.
